# WE'RE A FAMILY HERE (rewrite)

An SNL-style sketch. Third attempt, 2026-09-26.

The fix for "boring": the game mutates. The HR talk moves to the midpoint, then three weeks later a new hire out-Daves Dave, and Dave becomes Greg. The premise doesn't predict it. Dave's own lines come back as the button.

## CAST

- DAVE, 20s, earnest, new in town. Smiles like it's a strategy.
- GREG, 40s, middle manager. Dead inside, trying.
- JESS, 30s, Dave's deskmate. Tired. Responsible.
- MARCUS, 40s, coworker.
- TODD, 20s, bright, intense. Dave with the volume up.

## SKETCH

FADE IN:

INT. DATACORE OFFICES - CONFERENCE ROOM - MORNING

New-hire orientation. GREG by a PowerPoint: "WELCOME TO THE DATACORE FAMILY!" DAVE front row, notebook open, pen ready. Only one who brought a notebook.

GREG
...dental kicks in after ninety days, the break room fridge is a war zone, label your food. Last thing: here at Datacore, we're a family.

DAVE
(near tears)
You don't know how long I've waited to hear that.

GREG
...Okay. Real questions?

DAVE
When's Thanksgiving? I need to plan my casserole.

JESS
(under her breath)
Oh no.

CUT TO:

INT. OPEN OFFICE - LATER

DAVE's desk: a framed printout of the company staff page.

JESS
Is that us? From the website?

DAVE
Family photo. Getting it framed properly once I know everyone's middle names.

DAVE
(calling across the office)
Right, Dad?

GREG
(not looking up)
Don't call me Dad, Dave.

DAVE
Sorry.
(beat)
Pops?

MARCUS takes the good stapler from the supply shelf.

DAVE
(loud, tattling)
Dad! Marcus took the good stapler!

MARCUS
I'm forty-one.

DAVE
And I'm the baby, so.

CUT TO:

INT. BREAK ROOM - LATER

A casserole dish: "FOR THE FAMILY - LOVE, DAVE."

DAVE
It's green bean. My actual mom's recipe. She's not with us.

JESS
Oh. I'm sorry.

DAVE
She's in Ohio.

DAVE
(turning to JESS, sincere)
You've got eldest daughter energy, by the way. Tired. Responsible. Slightly angry at Dad.

JESS
(despite herself)
...Thank you?

CUT TO:

INT. GREG'S OFFICE - LATER

GREG holds a hand-drawn FAMILY TREE. The org chart, redrawn. Dave at the bottom in a crayon star: "THE BABY."

GREG
Dave. I need you to hear me. We're not a family. It's just something companies say.

DAVE
(quiet)
Yeah. That's what my dad said. Right before the divorce.

GREG
...I'm sorry.

DAVE
It's fine. I get it now.

He seems to mean it.

CUT TO:

INT. CONFERENCE ROOM - THREE WEEKS LATER

New-hire orientation. DAVE sits in back. No notebook. He's adjusted. GREG at the PowerPoint.

GREG
...label your food. Last thing: here at Datacore, we're a family.

TODD
(near tears)
You don't know how long I've waited to hear that.

DAVE freezes.

GREG
...Okay. Real questions?

TODD
When's Thanksgiving? I already rented a van. Twelve-seater. I need a headcount by tonight.

DAVE
(whisper)
No.

TODD
(calling across)
Right, Dad?

GREG
(slow smile, not looking up)
Don't call me Dad, Todd.

TODD
Sorry.
(beat)
Pops?

GREG looks at DAVE, delighted and dead-eyed:

GREG
He's your problem now, son.

CUT TO:

INT. OPEN OFFICE - LATER

TODD's desk: a professionally framed staff photo, a seating chart, a phone showing a group chat labeled "FAMILY MEETING." DAVE watches, horrified.

TODD
(into phone)
Love you too, Mom. See you Thursday.
(hangs up, turns to JESS)
Other Mom. Rolls. Thursday.

JESS
(to DAVE)
He calls me Mom now.

TODD
(to DAVE)
Big brother! I made us friendship bracelets. Yours says "ELDEST SON."

DAVE
Todd, listen. "We're a family" is just something companies say. Like synergy.

TODD
(wounded)
Greg knows what it means.

DAVE
Greg doesn't know what anything means, Todd.

CUT TO:

INT. GREG'S OFFICE - LATER

DAVE sits across from GREG, holding TODD's family tree. Elaborate. Color-coded. Dave is on it as "BIG BROTHER."

DAVE
Todd. I need you to hear me. We're not a family. It's just something companies say.

TODD
(quiet)
Yeah.

DAVE
I'm sorry.

TODD
That's what my dad said. Right before the divorce.

(Beat.)

TODD
So who's bringing what Thursday? I'm doing the green bean thing. My actual mom's recipe. She's not with us.

DAVE
...

TODD
She's in Ohio.

DAVE
(broken, from somewhere far away)
I'll bring pie.

TODD
(lighting up)
See? Family.

BLACKOUT.
