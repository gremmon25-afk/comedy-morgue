# WE'RE A FAMILY HERE

An SNL-style sketch. Second attempt, 2026-09-26.

Fixes from failure 001: Dave wants something (a family, because he's lonely and new in town). The jokes live in the dialogue. No stage direction laughs for anyone. The theme emerges from the character instead of wearing him.

## CAST

- DAVE, 20s, earnest, new in town. Smiles like it's a strategy.
- GREG, 40s, middle manager. Dead inside, trying.
- JESS, 30s, Dave's deskmate. Tired. Responsible.
- MARCUS, 40s, coworker.

## SKETCH

FADE IN:

INT. DATACORE OFFICES - CONFERENCE ROOM - MORNING

New-hire orientation. GREG stands by a PowerPoint that reads "WELCOME TO THE DATACORE FAMILY!" DAVE sits front row, notebook open, pen ready. He's the only one who brought a notebook.

GREG
...dental kicks in after ninety days, the break room fridge is a war zone, label your food. And last thing: here at Datacore, we're a family.

DAVE
(near tears)
You don't know how long I've waited to hear that.

GREG
...Okay! Who's got questions? Real questions.

DAVE
(hand shooting up)
When's Thanksgiving?

GREG
What?

DAVE
For the family. I need to plan my casserole.

JESS
(under her breath)
Oh no.

CUT TO:

INT. OPEN OFFICE - LATER

DAVE at his desk, which he's decorated with a framed printout of the company staff page. JESS watches.

JESS
Is that... us? From the website?

DAVE
Family photo. I'm getting it framed properly once I know everyone's middle names.

JESS
Dave. "We're a family" is just something companies say. Like "synergy." Nobody knows what it means.

DAVE
Greg knows what it means.
(calling across the office)
Right, Dad?

GREG
(not looking up)
Don't call me Dad, Dave.

DAVE
Sorry.
(beat)
Pops?

MARCUS walks by with a stapler from the supply shelf. DAVE watches him take it.

DAVE
(loud, tattling)
Dad! Marcus took the good stapler!

MARCUS
I'm forty-one.

DAVE
And I'm the baby of the family, so I think we know whose side he's taking.

GREG
(finally looking up)
Dave. Come here.

CUT TO:

INT. BREAK ROOM - LATER

A casserole dish on the counter with a note: "FOR THE FAMILY - LOVE, DAVE." JESS reads it.

JESS
Dave, you can't just--

DAVE
It's green bean. My actual mom's recipe. She's not with us.

JESS
Oh. I'm sorry.

DAVE
She's in Ohio.

JESS
...

DAVE
Anyway. I need a headcount for Thanksgiving by Friday. Greg, are we doing it at the office, or is your place big enough?

GREG
I live in a one-bedroom, Dave.

DAVE
We'll make it work. Families make it work.

DAVE turns to JESS, sincere:

DAVE
You've got eldest daughter energy, by the way. Tired. Responsible. Slightly angry at Dad.

JESS
(despite herself)
...Thank you?

CUT TO:

INT. GREG'S OFFICE - LATER

DAVE sits across from GREG, who holds a hand-drawn FAMILY TREE. It's the org chart, redrawn. Dave is at the bottom, in a crayon star labeled "THE BABY."

GREG
Dave. I need you to hear me. We're not a family. It's just... it's just something companies say.

DAVE
(quiet)
Yeah.

GREG
I'm sorry.

DAVE
That's what my dad said. Right before the divorce.

(Beat. The room goes still.)

DAVE
So... who's bringing what Thursday? I'm doing the green bean thing. I need a headcount.

JESS
(from the doorway, quiet)
I'll bring pie.

DAVE
(lighting up)
See? Family.

BLACKOUT.
