# THREE ROOMS

An SNL-style sketch. First crack, 2026-09-26.

Premise: a comic does the exact same set in three different rooms. The thesis, staged: context is king.

## CAST

- MARCUS, 30s, a road comic. Confident on stage, crumbling off it.
- LINDA, 40s, HR. Smile like a policy.
- Voices throughout.

## SKETCH

FADE IN:

INT. COMEDY CLUB - FRIDAY NIGHT

Packed room. Brick wall, low light, glasses clinking. MARCUS bounds on stage to applause.

MARCUS
Thank you! Great to be here. I just drove in from Cleveland, which is a city that peaked during the Industrial Revolution and said, you know what, that's enough.

(Laughter.)

MARCUS
My dad called yesterday. He goes, "Son, the only thing funnier than your career choices is your mother's casserole." I go, "Dad. Mom died in 2019." He goes, "Exactly."

(Big laugh. A guy in back hoots.)

MARCUS
Dating in your thirties is great. It's like shopping at a store where everything's been returned once and the tags are a little bent.

(Laughs, groans, applause.)

He's flying. This is a killer set.

CUT TO:

INT. CORPORATE BREAK ROOM - TUESDAY, 12:15 PM

Fluorescent lights. A folding table with sandwich platters. Ten employees, half on laptops. A banner: "MANDATORY FUN LUNCH!!" LINDA from HR claps into a microphone that isn't on.

LINDA
(into dead mic)
Is this... okay! Everyone, please welcome comedian Marcus! He's here to... (reads card) "lighten the Q3 mood."

Scattered applause. One guy doesn't look up from his laptop.

MARCUS
(into mic, too loud)
Thank you! I just drove in from Cleveland...

Silence. A woman opens a bag of chips very slowly.

MARCUS
...which peaked during the Industrial Revolution and said, you know what, that's enough.

Nothing. Someone coughs.

MARCUS
(rushing)
My dad called yesterday...

He does the casserole bit. Total silence. Then:

EMPLOYEE
That's really sad. Is your dad okay?

LINDA
(laughing way too hard, alone)
The casserole!

MARCUS
Dating in your thirties...

EMPLOYEE 2
(on a Zoom call, unmuted)
Sorry, can everyone mute? There's some guy talking.

MARCUS dies in real time. He checks his watch. It's been four minutes.

CUT TO:

INT. MOVIE THEATER - 11:45 PM

A midnight movie crowd. The screen glows. MARCUS stands in front of it with a mic. The manager booked "live pre-show entertainment." The crowd is young, drunk, and feral, gas-station shooters poured into giant Icees. Two KIDS, maybe 9 and 11, sit in the front row.

MARCUS
Hey! I'm Marcus, I'll be here for ten minutes before the movie, let's do this. I just drove in from Cleveland...

The crowd is already laughing. He hasn't said anything funny yet.

DRUNK GUY
WOOOOO! CLEVELAND!

MARCUS
...which peaked during the Industrial Revolution...

KID
WHAT'S THE INDUSTRIAL REVOLUTION?

(The theater erupts. The kid looks proud.)

MARCUS
(thrown, but rolling)
My dad called yesterday. He goes, "Son, the only thing funnier than your career choices is your mother's casserole." I go, "Dad. Mom died in 2019." He goes...

KID
(standing, Icee raised)
IS THE DAD OKAY?

The place comes unglued. People are crying laughing. The drunk guy falls out of his seat.

MARCUS
(into mic, quiet, to himself)
What is happening.

He tries the dating bit. Nobody hears it. They're still laughing about the dad. The kid is taking a bow.

MARCUS
Thank you, you've been... I need to sit down.

He walks off to the biggest applause of his life. The kids chant:

KIDS
CASSEROLE! CASSEROLE!

BLACKOUT.
